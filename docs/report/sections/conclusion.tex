\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presents a Vietnamese drug-information corpus, a 10{,}000-question retrieval benchmark and an agentic RAG pipeline for low-cost language models. Hybrid retrieval with the self-hosted Qwen3-Reranker-4B places a relevant chunk in the top ten for 97.5\% of the questions. Grounding cuts hallucinated answers eighteenfold with Gemini 3.8 Flash and twenty-five-fold with the open-weight Qwen3.5-9B, which then reaches a truthfulness of 0.913 against 0.923. Each agentic step pays off on the questions it is designed for. Rephrasing answers follow-up questions better than Concat RAG in both settings, with a truthfulness of 0.920 against 0.750 with Gemini, and the judge--refine loop completes loosely worded patient questions while spending almost all of its extra calls on the 39\% of questions whose first search is incomplete. These results demonstrate the feasibility of deploying agentic RAG with small open-weight models and self-hosted retrieval. However, the evaluation is limited by template-generated questions and assessment by a single pharmacist. Future work will incorporate real user questions and multiple expert evaluators, while exploring linear-algebraic logic programming~\cite{nguyen2022sparse,sakama2017linear} for explicit rule-based reasoning and verification of pharmaceutical information.
