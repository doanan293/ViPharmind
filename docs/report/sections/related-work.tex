\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) grounds language models in retrieved evidence~\cite{lewis2020rag}. Subsequent approaches introduce adaptive retrieval and reasoning mechanisms. Self-RAG uses reflection tokens to guide retrieval and generation~\cite{asai2024selfrag}, Corrective RAG triggers additional retrieval when evidence quality is insufficient~\cite{yan2024crag}, and Adaptive-RAG selects retrieval strategies based on question complexity~\cite{jeong2024adaptiverag}. ReAct-style agents interleave reasoning with tool use~\cite{yao2023react}. In contrast, our pipeline employs deterministic control flow and restricts each language model call to a small, structured decision, enabling agentic RAG with low-cost language models.

Retrieval choices have a strong influence on medical RAG. MIRAGE evaluates 41 combinations of corpora, retrievers and language models on 7{,}663 English medical questions and finds that the choice of corpus and retriever has a large effect on accuracy~\cite{xiong2024medrag}. Mikkelsen benchmarks ten embedding models and BM25 on clinical corpora and finds that corpus type, query format and chunking rival the effect of the model~\cite{mikkelsen2026clinical}. Nygren et al.\ answer drug side-effect questions with compact models and report accuracy together with latency~\cite{nygren2026drug}. General benchmarks such as BEIR~\cite{thakur2021beir}, MTEB~\cite{muennighoff2023mteb} and VN-MTEB~\cite{pham2025vnmteb} rank embedding models across many tasks, but none covers Vietnamese drug labelling or reports the cost of hybrid fusion and reranking alongside effectiveness. Our benchmark provides both.

The evaluation of RAG systems draws on complementary automatic measures, namely faithfulness from RAGAs~\cite{es2024ragas}, citation support from ALCE~\cite{gao2023alce}, nugget recall from the TREC 2024 RAG Track~\cite{pradeep2024nuggets}, the answer classes of CRAG, which penalise hallucinations more than abstentions~\cite{yang2024cragbench}, negative rejection from RGB~\cite{chen2024rgb} and prompt-injection success from AgentDojo~\cite{debenedetti2024agentdojo}. ARES calibrates LLM judges against a small human-labelled set through prediction-powered inference~\cite{saadfalcon2024ares,angelopoulos2023ppi}. Agentic pipelines are seldom assessed on all of these aspects together, which our evaluation does with paired ablations and a calibrated judge.

Prior work on Vietnamese medical question answering has mainly introduced datasets for general health information. ViMQ annotates patient questions with intents and entities~\cite{huy2021vimq}, ViHealthQA pairs health questions with expert answers~\cite{nguyen2022spbertqa}, and UIT-ViCoQA targets conversational reading comprehension of health news~\cite{luu2021vicoqa}. None addresses drug labelling or evaluates an agentic RAG system end to end.
