# Eval Data v3 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A correct 10,000-question benchmark whose rows carry construction-defined slices for every layer, and a 500-item golden set drawn from it, frozen only after audits pass.

**Architecture:** `evaluation assemble` gains a repair pass over the rule-built rows (drop pasted-text and heading-leak questions, re-point generic questions to the section that answers them, give pregnancy-and-lactation questions both sections, restore truncated product names), fixed generators for the added groups, and a `slices` field per row. Golden replaces outcome-selected probes with slice strata and is rebuilt from the repaired benchmark.

**Tech Stack:** pharma-lab (typer, pydantic), Fable 5.1 reviewers for audits and golden review, Kaggle for embeddings and reranking.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-18-eval-data-v3-design.md` (diagnosis, principles, groups, golden strata, reporting rules, freeze gates).

## Global Constraints

- Benchmark exactly 10,000 rows; golden 500, every item a benchmark row.
- Difficulty is defined from the question and the corpus, never from a system's result.
- Freeze gate: Fable audit of 25 rows per benchmark group, failure rate under 5%; slices must separate their layer (win rate inside at least twice outside, on `-v3-` runs).
- LLM calls only on golden; Jev on the 300 + 100 calibration rows is the declared exception; the calibration rows exclude golden sources and the parents of golden multi-turn items.
- Keep the query id of every rule-built row that the repair does not change, so caches carry over.
- No `# noqa`, no `# type: ignore`; ruff, pyrefly `--min-severity warn`, lint-imports, pytest pass.

## Review Focus

- A generic question re-pointed to a section that the document does not have must be dropped, not kept with its old gold.
- Pregnancy-and-lactation questions become multi_required only when both sections exist.
- Truncated-name repair must not touch names that end with a verb legitimately (only when the cut prefix is a prefix of the title and the next token is a question word).
- Colloquial aliases shared by several products become any_acceptable with every product's section, never a single arbitrary product.
- Slices are computed on the final row (after repair), not on the original.

### Task 1: Repair pass for the rule-built rows

**Files:** Create `pharma-lab/src/pharma_lab/e2e/benchmark_repair.py`; modify `benchmark_extension.py` (assemble calls repair before cutting); test `tests/e2e/test_benchmark_repair.py`.

**Interfaces:** Produces `repair_rows(rows: Sequence[dict], corpus: CorpusText) -> tuple[list[dict], Counter[str]]` returning repaired rows and a count per rule (`pasted_text`, `heading_leak`, `generic_regold`, `adr_regold`, `brand_regold`, `pregnancy_lactation`, `truncated_name`, `dropped_no_target`).

Rules (each with a test): drop a question without "?" of more than 10 words (pasted text); drop a question naming a section heading ("của mục", "trong mục"); a general-info question ("là thuốc gì", "là thuốc như thế nào", "Tìm thông tin về", "cần xem chuyên luận nào", "thông tin dược thư tổng quát") whose gold is `thong-tin-qui-che`, `tuong-ky`, `ten-thuong-mai` or `huong-dan-cach-xu-tri-adr` is re-pointed to the document's `thong-tin-chung` (dropped if absent); an ADR question whose gold is `huong-dan-cach-xu-tri-adr` is re-pointed to `tac-dung-khong-mong-muon-adr` (leaflet `tac-dung-phu`); a `ten-thuong-mai` gold stays only when a brand named in the question appears in the section, otherwise general-info rule; a question asking both pregnancy and lactation gets both sections and `multi_required` when the document has both; a leaflet question whose product prefix is a cut prefix of the title followed by a question word gets `brand_name(title)` instead.

### Task 2: Fixed generators for the added groups

**Files:** `golden_generate.py` (colloquial, glossary, multi_hop), `conversational.py`; tests in the matching test files.

- colloquial: alias → products map; an alias of one product keeps single gold; of several products → `answer_mode=any_acceptable`, `expected_section_ids` = that section of every product; at most two aliases per section; gold section of at least two sentences and 60 characters; aliases equal to the official product name (case-folded, in the title) are dropped.
- glossary: `GLOSSARY_PHRASES` extended to every glossary term with a natural Vietnamese phrase (≥ 15 terms); a term contributes at most 25% of the group; sections that already contain the phrase are skipped.
- conversational: parents are repaired rows with "?"; history siblings are repaired rows; the general-info noun phrase is not used in `intent_shift`/`noisy_followup`; dedupe on (last turn, gold); `topic_switch` rows carry the tag `control`.
- multi_hop: two_drug question neutral ("{A} và {B} dùng cùng nhau có tương tác không, và {intent of B}?"), names title-cased; `query_form` stays the kind, `eval_tags` gain `parallel` (two_drug), `sequential` (brand_to_ingredient), `multi_section` (three_intent).

### Task 3: Slices on every row

**Files:** `benchmark_extension.py` (`row_slices(row, corpus, chunks, families) -> list[str]`), test.

Slices: `header_dependent` (no gold section contains the first word of the document title), `low_overlap` (content-token overlap of question and gold sections + title under 0.35), `sibling_products` (the leaflet's active ingredient and asked section are shared by another leaflet of a different strength or form), `multi_chunk` (gold sections in two or more chunks). Assemble writes `slices` on every row.

### Task 4: Assemble and audit the benchmark

- [ ] Dry-run assemble; report repair counts and group sizes.
- [ ] Fable audit, 25 rows per group, rubric = realistic, understandable, gold answers it; record failure rate per group in the ledger.
- [ ] Fix rules and repeat until every group is under 5%.

### Task 5: Golden from the repaired benchmark

- Replace golden items whose benchmark row was dropped or re-pointed, and the items flagged by the review (3 colloquial, 2 glossary, 3 multi_turn).
- Replace `rerank_probe`, `lexical_probe`, `semantic_probe` with slice strata: `header_dependent` 20, `low_overlap` 20, `sibling_products` 20 (or `multi_chunk` if sibling does not separate rerank), drawn at random within the slice.
- Re-select colloquial 20 and glossary 10 at random from the fixed groups.
- multi_hop: sequential 42, parallel 30.
- Draft with the standard draft step, Fable review, independent audit, `golden build` against the benchmark.

### Task 6: Retrieval matrix `-v3-` and the separation check

Run the matrix (chain `v3-retrieval.sh`); compute the slice separation table; a slice that fails the 2× rule is reported as such and its golden stratum switches to the next slice.
